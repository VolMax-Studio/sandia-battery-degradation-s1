## 4. Estimator Status & Author-Defined Basis

### 4.1 Author-Defined EFC Basis (Preger et al., p. 120532-4)
- Preger et al. define EFC as:
  > *"In this work, one EFC is based on the nominal capacity of the cell. Therefore, for each cell, the total capacity throughput was divided by the nominal capacity to get the total equivalent full cycle count."*

### 4.2 Candidate Items Flagged for Phase 1 Preregistration Freeze:
1. **Candidate Throughput Formulations:** Discharge-only vs average cycle throughput.
2. **Baseline Capacity ($Q_0$):** Reference Performance Test (RPT) capacity check (3 cycles at 0.5C, 0–100% SOC) vs Cycle 1 discharge capacity.
3. **80% EOL Threshold Crossing ($N_{80}$):** Discrete first integer crossing ($Q_k \le 0.80 \times Q_0$) vs linear interpolation.
4. **Cohort Partitioning:** Separation of measured cells (with `+` points) from projected/extrapolated cells (without `+` points) per Figure 2a caption.
