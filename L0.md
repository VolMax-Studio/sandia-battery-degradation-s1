The full population of 86 SNL cells is cataloged in [`evidence/l0/raw_listing.txt`](evidence/l0/raw_listing.txt), generated from static `batteryarchive_metadata.csv` via [`evidence/l0/scripts/generate_listing.py`](evidence/l0/scripts/generate_listing.py).

### Identifier Standards & Literal Transformation:

- **Filesystem Filename Base:** `SNL_18650_{cathode}_{temperature}C_{min_soc}-{max_soc}_{charge_rate}-{discharge_rate}C_{replicate}`  
  *(where `/` in charge/discharge rate is converted to `-` per `data_transfer.py:L135`: `file_name = cell_id.replace("/", "-")`)*

### Breakdown by Cathode Chemistry:
1. **LFP (30 cells):** A123 APR18650M1A (1.1 Ah nominal, 3.3 V nominal)
   - Temperatures: `15C`, `25C`, `35C` | DOD: `0-100%`, `20-80%`, `40-60%` | Rates: `0.5C`, `1C`, `2C`, `3C`
2. **NMC (32 cells):** LG Chem 18650HG2 / commercial (3.0 Ah nominal, 3.6 V nominal)
   - Temperatures: `15C`, `25C`, `35C` | DOD: `0-100%`, `20-80%`, `40-60%` | Rates: `0.5C`, `1C`, `2C`, `3C`
3. **NCA (24 cells):** Panasonic NCR18650B (3.2 Ah nominal, 3.6 V nominal)
   - Temperatures: `15C`, `25C`, `35C` | DOD: `0-100%`, `20-80%`, `40-60%` | Rates: `0.5C`, `1C`, `2C` (3C excluded per mfg bounds)

> [!NOTE]
> As verified in `evidence/l0/raw_listing.txt` and Table II:
> - Partial depth-of-discharge conditions (`20-80%` and `40-60%` DOD) are evaluated exclusively at `25C` (non-full factorial design).
> - The `3C` discharge rate is evaluated for LFP and NMC, and is absent for NCA in the deposit.

## 4. Estimator Status & Author-Defined Basis

### 4.1 Author-Defined EFC Basis (Preger et al., p. 120532-4)
- Preger et al. define EFC as:
  > *"In this work, one EFC is based on the nominal capacity of the cell. Therefore, for each cell, the total capacity throughput was divided by the nominal capacity to get the total equivalent full cycle count."*

### 4.2 Candidate Items Flagged for Phase 1 Preregistration Freeze:
1. **Candidate Throughput Formulations:** Discharge-only vs average cycle throughput.
2. **Baseline Capacity ($Q_0$):** Reference Performance Test (RPT) capacity check (3 cycles at 0.5C, 0–100% SOC) vs Cycle 1 discharge capacity.
3. **80% EOL Threshold Crossing ($N_{80}$):** Discrete first integer crossing ($Q_k \le 0.80 \times Q_0$) vs linear interpolation.
4. **Cohort Partitioning:** Separation of measured cells (with `+` points) from projected/extrapolated cells (without `+` points) per Figure 2a caption.

### 4.3 Live Dataset Invariant & 2020 Observation Horizon
As recorded in [`evidence/l0/source_pages/batteryarchive_study_summaries.html`](evidence/l0/source_pages/batteryarchive_study_summaries.html#L121):
> *"The dataset from Sandia National Labs used in the publication “Degradation of Commercial Lithium-ion Cells as a Function of Chemistry and Cycling Conditions” consists of commercial 18650 NCA, NMC, and LFP cells cycled to 80% capacity (although cycling is still ongoing)."*

Because cycling continued post-publication, telemetry accumulated past the 2020 publication data horizon must be censored at the frozen 2020 cutoff for all Figure 2a comparison targets (Target A1 and Target A2).
